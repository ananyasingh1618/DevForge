Everything below is fictional or generated at recording time. Nothing personal, no credentials.

## What was not used

- No GitHub token, no repository connection, no `.env` content, no API keys on screen.
- No temporary public tunnel URL. Recording was made against the local production-style stack on `127.0.0.1`.

## Cleanup

After recording, all `demo.*@example.com` users were deleted from the recording database (their projects and versions cascade-delete) and the copied evaluation row was removed. Earlier unrelated test users from smoke tests were left untouched.

## How the video was assembled (honesty notes)

- The requirements, PRD, architecture, epics and tasks requests were real and ran against the live AI provider in one continuous session: 17, 21, 25, 20 and 27 seconds.
- Those five waits are shown at 4x speed with an orange on-screen banner stating the real wait time. All other footage is real time.
- The Evaluations detail segment was recorded in a separate short session and spliced in, because the first take spent time on a wrong selector.
- Earlier recording attempts hit transient provider errors (HTTP 502 and a free-tier rate limit). They are not in the video; the assembled footage is from one clean run.
